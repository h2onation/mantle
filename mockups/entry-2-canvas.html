How this works

You talk. It listens.

Bring a real moment. Jove asks about it the way a sharp friend would — no forms, no quiz, no advice you didn’t ask for.

It builds a manual of how you operate.

Over time, the through-lines become a short document about how you actually work — in your own words.

Nothing’s saved unless you say so.

Jove only proposes. You confirm what’s true, reword what’s close, and drop what isn’t you. It’s not therapy and it won’t diagnose you.
