A note from Jove

You don’t have to
arrive ready.

I’m Jove. There’s nothing to set up here, and no right way to begin. We just talk — about whatever’s real for you — and slowly a picture comes together of how you actually work.

It’s easier to see someone else’s patterns than your own. I’ll be the outside view — the strengths you’ve stopped noticing, the loops you keep landing in. You decide what’s true.

  • No forms, no questionnaires — only conversation.
  • Your pace. Stop, drift, come back whenever.
  • Nothing is saved unless you say yes to it.
  • No judgment, no fixing. Just being seen clearly.

Whenever you’re ready, I’ll be here. — Jove
