Desktop directions.

Three ways to grow mywalnut past the phone frame. Each mockup has a working theme toggle (bottom-left, or the left rail in option 3).

Option 1 · The safe floor

The Folio — refined single column

The conversation becomes the page. Fixed 66-character measure, larger type, marginalia in the side margins (timestamps, checkpoint marks), a writing-slab composer. The Manual stays behind a quiet top-right link. Zero disclosure risk.

Option 2 · Recommended

The Study — conversation + Manual spine

Same reading column, plus the Manual as a closed book spine on the desk's edge — layer numerals and entry pips, no content. It opens on demand (click, ⌘M) or when a checkpoint lands, and the confirmed entry visibly travels into its layer. Esc closes it.

Option 3 · The far end

The Studio — session rail + conversation + Manual

The full desktop app: sessions as a table of contents on the left, conversation centered, Manual always open on the right. Most capable, most "workstation" — the document watches the conversation, which is exactly its risk.
