How this works

You talk. Jove listens, and writes back what it heard.

It’s a conversation, not a form

No quiz, no test, no profile to fill out. You bring a moment; Jove asks about it.

Nothing’s saved unless you confirm it

When Jove finds something worth keeping, it asks first. You decide what becomes yours.

It’s not therapy

Jove won’t diagnose you or tell you what to do. It helps you put words to how you already work.
