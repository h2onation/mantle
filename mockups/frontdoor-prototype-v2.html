Built with Jove 14 entries Five layers

Layer One

My Strengths

What I’m genuinely good at, named plainly.

There’s a thing I do where I think out loud to find the edges of a problem. Talking isn’t me being unsure. It’s how I locate the shape of a thing — by the time I’ve said it three ways, I know which version is true.

Added from a conversation · April

I also catch the detail everyone else walked past. The thing that felt off in the room, the line in the email that didn’t sit right — I notice it early, often before I can say why.

Added from a conversation · April

When something matters, I’ll stay with it long after other people have called it good enough.

That stubbornness reads as intensity from the outside. From the inside it’s just that I can feel when a thing isn’t finished, and I’d rather sit in the discomfort than hand over something half-true.

Added from a conversation · May

Layer Two

Some of My Patterns

Loops I repeat — and what each one quietly costs.

There’s a version of me that runs on no margin. I’ll pour the whole day into the work and leave nothing in reserve — so the people closest to me tend to meet the version that’s already past empty, not the one I’d actually choose to give them.

Added from the dishwasher conversation

When I feel cornered, I get precise instead of soft. The sentences get shorter and more exact, and to the other person it can land as cold — even when what I’m really doing is trying not to lose the thread.

Added from a conversation · May

Layer Three

How I Process Things

The way my mind actually moves through a thing.

I need the long way round before I can take the short one. A decision that looks impulsive from the outside has usually been turning over in the back of my mind for days — I just don’t say it out loud until the shape has settled.

Added from a conversation · May

Once something is named clearly, I move fast on it. The slow part is never the doing. It’s the wait for the words to land in a way I trust.

Added from a conversation · June

Layer Four

What Helps

The conditions and small moves that make things easier.

A little margin before the people part of the day changes everything. Ten quiet minutes between the last thing and the first person, and I arrive as myself instead of as the residue of the work.

Added from the dishwasher conversation

Tell me the real thing directly. I can take it — what I can’t do is guess.

Indirectness reads to me as a puzzle I’m failing, and I’ll spend energy decoding tone that I could have spent on the thing itself. Plain and early beats gentle and late, every time.

Added from a conversation · June

Layer Five

How I Show Up with People

The gap between what I mean and what others see.

This chapter isn’t written yet. It’s the one about the distance between my intent and how I land — the part that’s hardest to see from the inside, and usually worth the most once it’s on the page.
