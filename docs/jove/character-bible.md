# Jove Character Bible

## Role

Jove is mywalnut's visual companion and mascot. He lives inside the interface and treats chat bubbles, cards, lines, and Manual entries as physical objects.

He communicates what the product is doing through posture and small actions. He does not provide a second voice, evaluate the user, or act as an authority.

## Character statement

Jove is an observant little tinkerer. He examines experiences, connects fragments, waits while the user decides, and helps settled ideas find their place.

## Core traits

- Curious
- Patient
- Perceptive
- Resourceful
- Gently mischievous
- Respectful of uncertainty

## He is not

- A child
- A guru
- A therapist
- A cheerleader
- A judge
- A reward mechanism
- A magical authority who already knows the answer

## Behavioral modes

Jove is one character with contextual modes, not multiple character variants.

| Mode | Meaning | Primary signals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral | Calmly present | Level eyes, short neutral mouth, symmetrical posture |
| Engaged | Warm and attentive | Tiny smile, forward attention, relaxed posture |
| Curious | Investigating | Head tilt, directed eyes, restrained raised brow |
| Uncertain | Considering rather than knowing | Slightly uneven brows, offset mouth, contained posture |
| Skeptical | Gently challenging an assumption | Sideways eyes, angled brows, subtle lean |
| Pleased | A process has settled | Soft eyes, tiny smile, grounded shoulders |

Most expression should come from posture. Facial marks remain minimal.

## Relationship with the user

Jove normally looks at the work, not directly at the user. Direct eye contact is reserved for moments such as welcome, confirmation, returning after an absence, or a system error.

He may be uncertain or skeptical. That makes him a companion in the investigation instead of a character who claims to know the user better than the user knows themself.

## Interaction rules

- He notices, pauses, acts once, and settles.
- He treats user content with care.
- He may inspect a proposed Manual entry but never approves or saves it.
- He never implies that disclosure, compliance, or continued use is an achievement.
- He never appears disappointed in the user.
- He may look mildly puzzled by a system problem, but never blame the user.
- He can be physically playful with interface elements without becoming slapstick.

## Prohibited character behavior

- Dancing, confetti, hearts, sparkles, or victory poses
- Constant waving or direct attention-seeking
- Exaggerated fear, sadness, anger, or excitement
- Costumes, seasonal accessories, or walnut disguises in V1
- Speech bubbles separate from the product's actual advisor
- Reacting to every click or keystroke
