For the care and operation of one particular human. Written in their own words, entered only with their approval.

01

My Strengths

LAYER I · 2 ENTRIES

Before you open a file or write a line, the structure assembles itself somewhere behind your eyes. From outside, the stillness reads as not working. The stillness is the work. By the time your hands move, most of the decisions are already made. It's why the work holds up under pressure that should have sunk it.

02

Some of My Patterns

LAYER II · 1 ENTRY

In the moment, the answer people want to hear leaves your mouth before the calendar gets a vote. It isn't people-pleasing exactly. The room has a rhythm and you match it. The cost arrives later, addressed to a version of you who was never in the meeting.

04

What Helps

LAYER IV · 1 ENTRY

You can't start alone at two weeks out. You can start next to someone. It doesn't matter if they're working on something else entirely. Their presence makes the work real the way a deadline does, without the dread. The engine turns over early and quietly.

05

How I Show Up with People

LAYER V · 1 ENTRY

In a group your face goes still and your words go short. From outside it can read as checked out. It's the opposite. Everything is arriving at once and you're routing it. The people who know you well have learned that the quiet is where you do your part of the conversation.
